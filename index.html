How To Use This Study

Each lesson includes six parts:

  1. Teaching Note
  2. Scripture
  3. What This Means
  4. Reflect
  5. Pray
  6. Listen

This study is not designed to be rushed. Move through each lesson slowly. Spend time talking with God and listening to Him.

Hearing God's Voice

"My sheep hear My voice, and I know them, and they follow Me."
John 10:27

Prayer is not only talking to God. Prayer is also listening.

Most Christians do not hear an audible voice. God often guides us through Scripture, conviction, wisdom, reminders of truth, other believers, and thoughts impressed upon our hearts.

When a thought comes while praying:

  • Write it down.
  • Ask: Does this agree with Scripture?
  • Ask: Does this sound like Jesus?
  • Discuss important impressions with a mature believer.

Over time you will become more familiar with God's leading. Keep a journal and record what you believe God may be teaching you.

Life and Purpose

A New Creation • 2 Corinthians 5:17

Teaching Note

When a person trusts in Jesus Christ, God does not simply improve the old life. He creates something new.

Many people define themselves by their mistakes, achievements, failures, relationships, or what others think about them. God defines believers differently.

In Christ, your identity begins with what God says about you.

Scripture

2 Corinthians 5:17 (NKJV)

Add the NKJV verse text here.

What This Means

Being a new creation does not mean becoming perfect overnight.

  • God has given you new spiritual life.
  • Your relationship with Him has changed.
  • Your future has changed.
  • Your identity has changed.

The Christian life is not about becoming someone else. It is about becoming who God says you already are in Christ.

Reflect

  • How do I usually describe myself?
  • What labels from my past still affect me?
  • What would change if I truly believed God has made me new?

Pray

Father, thank You for making me a new creation in Christ. Help me stop defining myself by my failures and start believing what You say about me. Teach me to walk in the new life You have given me. Amen.

Listen

Take two quiet minutes.

Ask:

Lord, what do You want me to understand about my new identity?

Write down:

  • Thoughts
  • Scriptures
  • Questions
  • Convictions

Then ask:

  • Does this align with Scripture?
  • Does this sound like Jesus?
  • Should I discuss this with someone helping me follow Christ?
God's Workmanship • Ephesians 2:10

Teaching Note

Many people spend their lives trying to prove their value. They chase achievement, approval, success, popularity, or status hoping those things will tell them who they are.

God starts in a different place.

Before talking about what you do, God tells you who you are. You are His workmanship. You are not an accident, a mistake, or an afterthought. God intentionally created you and has prepared a purpose for your life.

Scripture

Ephesians 2:10 (NKJV)

"For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand that we should walk in them."

What This Means

The Christian life is not about earning God's approval through good works.

God saves us first. Then He invites us to participate in the good works He has already prepared.

  • God created you intentionally.
  • God has a purpose for your life.
  • Your value comes from belonging to Him.
  • Good works flow from salvation, not toward salvation.

You do not have to invent your purpose. Instead, you learn to walk with God and discover the purpose He has already prepared.

Reflect

  • Where do I usually look for value or significance?
  • Do I believe God created me with purpose?
  • How might my life change if I viewed myself as God's workmanship?

Pray

Father, thank You for creating me intentionally and giving my life purpose. Help me stop measuring my worth by achievements or the opinions of others. Teach me to walk faithfully in the good works You have prepared for me. Amen.

Listen

Take two quiet minutes.

Lord, what purpose are You helping me discover right now?

Write down:

  • People God may be drawing your attention toward.
  • Strengths He has given you.
  • Opportunities to serve others.
  • Questions you want to explore further.

Review what you write and ask:

  • Does this align with Scripture?
  • Does this reflect the character of Jesus?
  • Should I discuss this with a mature believer?
Born Again • 1 Peter 1:23

Teaching Note

Jesus described salvation as being "born again." Christianity is not merely adopting new habits or joining a new community. God gives spiritual life to people who place their faith in Christ.

A believer receives a new beginning that originates with God.

Scripture

1 Peter 1:23 (NKJV)

"Having been born again, not of corruptible seed but incorruptible, through the word of God which lives and abides forever."

What This Means

  • Your spiritual life comes from God.
  • The new life God gives is lasting and secure.
  • God's Word plays a central role in spiritual growth.
  • You are part of a completely new family and future.

Being born again means your relationship with God has fundamentally changed.

Reflect

  • How has my life changed since I began following Jesus?
  • What old patterns is God helping me leave behind?
  • How am I allowing God's Word to shape me?

Pray

Father, thank You for giving me new life through Jesus Christ. Help me grow in faith and continue becoming the person You created me to be. Amen.

Listen

Ask:

Lord, what area of my life are You making new right now?
