How To Use This Study

Each lesson includes six parts:

  1. Teaching Note
  2. Scripture
  3. What This Means
  4. Reflect
  5. Pray
  6. Listen

This study is not designed to be rushed. Move through each lesson slowly. Spend time talking with God and listening to Him.

Hearing God's Voice

"My sheep hear My voice, and I know them, and they follow Me."
John 10:27

Prayer is not only talking to God. Prayer is also listening.

Most Christians do not hear an audible voice. God often guides us through Scripture, conviction, wisdom, reminders of truth, other believers, and thoughts impressed upon our hearts.

When a thought comes while praying:

  • Write it down.
  • Ask: Does this agree with Scripture?
  • Ask: Does this sound like Jesus?
  • Discuss important impressions with a mature believer.

Over time you will become more familiar with God's leading. Keep a journal and record what you believe God may be teaching you.

Life and Purpose

A New Creation • 2 Corinthians 5:17

Teaching Note

When a person trusts in Jesus Christ, God does not simply improve the old life. He creates something new.

Many people define themselves by their mistakes, achievements, failures, relationships, or what others think about them. God defines believers differently.

In Christ, your identity begins with what God says about you.

Scripture

2 Corinthians 5:17 (NKJV)

Add the NKJV verse text here.

What This Means

Being a new creation does not mean becoming perfect overnight.

  • God has given you new spiritual life.
  • Your relationship with Him has changed.
  • Your future has changed.
  • Your identity has changed.

The Christian life is not about becoming someone else. It is about becoming who God says you already are in Christ.

Reflect

  • How do I usually describe myself?
  • What labels from my past still affect me?
  • What would change if I truly believed God has made me new?

Pray

Father, thank You for making me a new creation in Christ. Help me stop defining myself by my failures and start believing what You say about me. Teach me to walk in the new life You have given me. Amen.

Listen

Take two quiet minutes.

Ask:

Lord, what do You want me to understand about my new identity?

Write down:

  • Thoughts
  • Scriptures
  • Questions
  • Convictions

Then ask:

  • Does this align with Scripture?
  • Does this sound like Jesus?
  • Should I discuss this with someone helping me follow Christ?
